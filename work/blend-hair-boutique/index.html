Blend Hair Boutique

blendhairboutique.com

The largest build the studio has shipped. A trilingual site for a Brazilian owned salon in Plantation, Florida, where every page ends at the same place: a booking made without anyone picking up the phone.

Colour and type

Drawn around an existing mark

The script mark was already theirs; everything around it was not. The site's system was drawn to sit under that mark without competing with it: a warm white ground, parchment for the quiet sections, a single gold used as metal rather than as decoration, and a high-contrast garalde for display against a neutral grotesque for everything you actually have to read.

Three languages, one site

English, Portuguese, Spanish

The owners are Brazilian, most of the team speaks more than one language, and so does Plantation. The site is built in English, Portuguese and Spanish from the same source, each language served at its own path and declared to Google with hreflang, so a search in any of the three lands on a page written in it rather than on a machine translation of the wrong one.

en-USEnglish The default, and the x-default for everything outside the other two. pt-BRPortuguês Written for the Brazilian community the salon was built around, not translated at it. esEspañol The third language of the county, and of a good share of the chairs.

Every page ends in a booking

Fresha, WhatsApp, phone

A salon site has one job. There are eight service pages, covering haircuts, colour, balayage and highlights, styling, smoothing, hair care, nails and bridal. Each one explains a single treatment and hands straight off to the booking system, with WhatsApp and the phone kept one tap away for anyone who would rather ask first.

BookingFresha Live availability and instant confirmation, reached from the header, every service page and the close of the home page.
DirectWhatsApp & phone The two channels this clientele actually uses, on the contact section and the sticky bar.
Also builtGift cards, blog, FAQ The pages that catch the searches the service pages do not.

Local search

Plantation, Florida

Blend had already earned the reviews. The site's job was to make them legible to a search engine: HairSalon, BeautySalon and LocalBusiness structured data carrying the address, hours, service list and aggregate rating, a service page per treatment to answer the searches people actually type, and a reviews page that shows the rating rather than claiming it.

Rating4.9 ★ · 1,230+ Google reviews, surfaced on the page and declared in the structured data.
Service pagesEight One per treatment, each its own indexable answer to a different query.
Open since2018 Kérastase salon, Brazilian smoothing specialists, eight master stylists.

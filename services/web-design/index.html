Capability / Web design and build

Web design and development in Miami

Every site the studio ships is written by hand in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No page builder, no purchased theme, no plugin stack to maintain. That is not nostalgia. It is the only way to control what the page weighs, how fast it paints and what a search engine can read.

What hand-coded actually buys you

Against builders and themes

A Wix or Squarespace page ships a framework, a theme, and the code paths for every feature you are not using. A WordPress build adds a plugin for each thing the theme forgot, and each plugin adds its own stylesheet, its own script and its own update risk. You pay for that in load time on every visit, and in maintenance every month.

A hand-written page ships only what is on it. The home page of this site is around 250KB before images, and every byte of it is doing something. That margin is what buys you the video hero, the photography at full quality, and the motion, without the page feeling slow.

The other half is control. Structured data, heading order, image formats and crawl paths are decisions here, not settings buried in a theme you do not own.

What a build includes

Six parts, one pair of hands

The studio does not hand off between a designer, a developer and a copywriter, which is where most agency projects lose their shape. The same person draws it, writes it and builds it.

01 Art direction What the site looks like and why, decided before a line of it is built.
02 Design Type, colour, spacing and motion, drawn against real content rather than placeholder text.
03 Copy The words on the page, written to be read and to be found.
04 Front-end build Hand-written HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Responsive, accessible, tested on real devices.
05 Search groundwork Semantic markup, structured data, metadata, sitemap, and compressed media.
06 Launch and handover Deployment, analytics, and a walkthrough so you can run it.

Built for how people actually arrive

Mobile, multilingual, bookable

Most local traffic is a phone, held one-handed, on a connection that is worse than yours. Everything here is designed at that size first and widened afterwards, not the other way round.

Where an audience is not monolingual, the site is built in more than one language from the same source and declared to Google with hreflang, so a search in Portuguese or Spanish lands on a page written in it. Blend Hair Boutique runs in English, Portuguese and Spanish that way. So does this one.

And where the business takes appointments or orders, the booking system is wired in rather than linked to as an afterthought, because a site that ends in a phone number loses the people who will not call.

Who this is for

And who it is not

It suits a business that sells something specific to a definable audience and has been let down by a template: a salon, a studio, a practice, a maker, a local service that competes on craft rather than price.

It does not suit a project that needs fifty pages next month, a marketplace, or a team that wants to edit the layout weekly. The studio takes one project at a time, which is the reason the work looks the way it does and also the reason it is not the right answer for everyone.

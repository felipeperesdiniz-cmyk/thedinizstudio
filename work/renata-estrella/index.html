Identity, colour and type

Built from nothing

Everything here was drawn for her: the monogram, the palette and the type system. A near-black ground so the gold reads as metal rather than yellow, three weights of that gold for depth, and a serif-and-grotesk pairing that carries from the site into the e-book and the Google listing.
